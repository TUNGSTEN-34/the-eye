Resources

Practical starting points for building research skills and making better use of information.

⌕

Research basics

Define your question, find primary sources, compare accounts, and keep track of where claims came from.

▤

Data literacy

Read charts carefully, check units and dates, look for missing context, and distinguish correlation from causation.

◈

Digital awareness

Check URLs and authors, beware of sensational wording, and verify surprising claims before reposting.

About the platform

Curious minds. Clearer understanding.

The Eye is an information platform project under the Analyst identity. Its goal is to bring public-interest topics, educational explainers, and useful data into one clear, approachable space.

We value transparency: sources should be visible, opinions should be distinguishable from facts, and demo features should never be presented as fully operational services.
