Launch requirements

  • Secure sign-up and sign-in for each customer.
  • Server-side admin role and protected owner console.
  • Shared database for inventory, purchases, accounts, and audit logs.
  • Atomic stock deduction so the same unit cannot be assigned twice.
  • Clear contracts explaining whether the arrangement is machine rental, revenue sharing, or a financial investment.
  • Verified legal and regulatory review before handling real funds or advertising returns.
